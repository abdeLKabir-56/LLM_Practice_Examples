
With the advancements in Generative AI, AI Agents will be key in reshaping the business. The AI Agents are powerful tools that are making processes simpler and data more consumable for all - small, medium and large enterprises.

AI Agents
AI Agents are software tools which can orchestrate tasks autonomously by making decisions based on its environment and inputs, with preset guard-rails. They are advanced systems that can learn and update their behaviour to achieve the expected outcome, if given the context and the objective/goal to achieve. While we are at the very early stages of the Agent realm, evaluation and tracking are essential for an accurate and responsible response.

Agents are given tasks, data and the necessary tools to perform a task with preset guidelines in the prompt. Prompt Engineering is umpteen import to get the right set of responses in a situation where the Agent has found the answer or is not in a position to answer due to data unavailability or due to inappropriate queries. Usually Agents perform actions based on the Role assigned.

In a Multi-Agent System (MAS), there are multiple agents who work towards a common goal. They may work as sequential set of agents or may have a hierarchy of agents to supervise and achieve the goal assigned.

Agentic Workflows
We have known workflow orchestration tools from the early years in Technology. These tools help us to orchestrate a sequence of steps to achieve an end goal. Similarly Agentic Workflows with AI Agents are capable of learning and performing complex tasks with higher accuracy. While we use Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs) in Data pipelines, in AI, there is a need for allowing loops to help the system to monitor, self-learn, iterate over the tasks and produce an efficient outcome. AI Agentic workflows manage the task execution order, assign responsibilities to specified AI Agents and review the responses to ensure an acceptable outcome for complex goals and tasks.

Good Frameworks
There are a plethora of frameworks coming up to help build robust Agent workflows today. Autogen, Crew AI, LangGraph from Langchain have quite a presence in terms of building Agentic Workflows. While it is much easier to control the workflow, memory and other optimizations with Langraph, it comes with a short learning curve. Autogen and Crew AI on the other hand are easier to implement but act like a blackbox in terms of debugging or tweaking the flow as per our need. It is difficult to predict execution flow until you are aware of the internal working and implementation for these frameworks. Today we will discuss a simple workflow with Crew AI just to understand how we can set up an agentic workflow with different AI Agents.
